AgentPay Go-to-Market Plan

"Ramp for AI Agents" — Financial rails for autonomous AI

Positioning Statement

"The first financial infrastructure built for AI agents. Issue virtual cards, set spending limits, and track expenses—all through APIs your agents can use. No human intervention required."

Key Value Props:

  • Instant financial access: Virtual cards ready in seconds via API
  • Built-in guardrails: Smart budgets and spending limits prevent runaway costs
  • Agent-native: RESTful APIs designed for programmatic access
  • Enterprise-ready: Compliance, security, and audit trails from day one

Target Personas

Primary: AI Startup CTOs

Who: Technical leaders building AI agent platforms

  • Pain: Need to give agents spending capability without financial risk
  • Job-to-be-done: "Enable my agents to make purchases without manual approval"
  • Channels: AI Twitter, Hacker News, developer Slack communities

Secondary: Automation Agency Founders

Who: Service providers deploying AI agents for clients

  • Pain: Managing expenses across multiple client agent deployments
  • Job-to-be-done: "Isolate and track spending per client agent"
  • Channels: LinkedIn, automation-focused communities, referrals

Tertiary: Enterprise AI/Engineering Leaders

Who: Large company teams experimenting with AI agents

  • Pain: Corporate cards too slow, expense reports don't work for agents
  • Job-to-be-done: "Get AI agent spending approved by finance"
  • Channels: Industry conferences, enterprise sales (future), content marketing

Launch Strategy

Phase 1: Developer Community Validation (Month 1)

Goal: 100 developer signups, 10 active customers

Tactics:

  • Ship to Product Hunt with AI agent angle
  • Seed AI Twitter with technical threads
  • Launch on Hacker News as "Show HN"
  • Direct outreach to 50 AI startup CTOs

Phase 2: Content Authority (Months 2-3)

Goal: 500 signups, establish thought leadership

Tactics:

  • Weekly AI infrastructure deep-dives
  • Open source agent financial toolkit
  • Speaking at AI conferences
  • Partner integrations (LangChain, etc.)

Phase 3: Scale Distribution (Months 4-6)

Goal: 2,000 signups, viral growth loops

Tactics:

  • Built-in referral mechanics
  • API-first marketing (usage drives discovery)
  • SEO dominance for "AI agent payments"
  • Paid acquisition on proven channels

Competitive Differentiation

vs. Traditional Corporate Cards

  • Speed: Cards issued instantly via API, not 2-week approval
  • Automation: No manual expense reports or approval workflows
  • Integration: RESTful APIs, not clunky web portals

vs. Stripe/Other Payment APIs

  • Spend controls: Built-in budgets and spending limits
  • Agent-specific: Designed for autonomous, not human usage
  • Visibility: Real-time spending dashboards for agent operators
